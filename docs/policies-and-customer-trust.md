## 2. Verified Policy Details

### A. Refund & Replacement Policy
- **Digital Product Delivery Standard:** Every template sold is delivered as a complete, unbundled digital file package via Google Drive. Because digital files cannot be physically returned once delivered, PortfolioStore 99 does not offer refunds after delivery for changes of mind.
- **Fair One-Time Replacement Option:**
  - If a customer purchases a template and realizes a different template from the catalog better fits their needs, they may exchange it under the replacement policy.
  - The customer pays **50% of the new template's listed price** to receive the new codebase.
  - *Example:* If a customer buys a ₹99 template and wants to switch to a ₹199 template, they pay ₹99.50 (50% of ₹199) for the replacement.
  - **Limit:** This replacement option is permitted **only once per order**.
  - **Timeframe:** Replacement requests must be submitted within **7 days** of the initial purchase.
- **Defective or Corrupted Deliverables Guarantee:**
  - If a customer receives corrupted files, an incorrect download link, or deliverables that do not match what was demonstrated in the live preview deployment at the time of purchase, PortfolioStore 99 resolves or replaces the files immediately at **no charge**. This resolution does not count against the customer's one-time replacement option.
- **How to Initiate a Replacement:**
  - Email `portfoliostore99@gmail.com` or message WhatsApp `@ayushcmd` with the original Order ID and the desired replacement template name.

### B. Terms of Service & Licensing
- **License Grant:** Each template purchase grants the customer a **single-user, perpetual, non-exclusive license** to:
  - Modify, customize, brand, and build upon the template source code.
  - Deploy the resulting website on any hosting provider (Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, AWS, GitHub Pages, or private servers) for a personal portfolio or client project.
- **Restrictions:**
  - The customer is strictly prohibited from reselling, sub-licensing, redistributing, publishing, or sharing the raw template source code or digital asset bundles in template marketplaces, free code repositories, or public repositories as a competing product.
- **Delivery Mechanism:**
  - Templates are delivered digitally via secure Google Drive access links provided immediately on the confirmation page (`/thank-you?order_id=...`) and sent via automated email receipt.
  - No physical merchandise is shipped.

### C. Privacy Policy & Data Collection
- **Information Collected:**
  - Customer Name and Email Address entered during checkout (used strictly to deliver digital files and provide transaction receipts).
  - Basic order metadata (template purchased, transaction amount, Razorpay order/payment identifiers).
  - WhatsApp chat contents and telephone numbers when a customer voluntarily reaches out via WhatsApp support.
- **Payment Credential Privacy:**
  - PortfolioStore 99 **never** collects, processes, or stores credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, netbanking passwords, or UPI PINs.
  - All payment data is collected and processed exclusively by **Razorpay** under its certified PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.
- **Zero Advertising or Tracking Cookies:**
  - The website does not employ third-party tracking pixels, marketing cookies, or behavioral advertising trackers. Any cookies present are strictly functional (e.g., maintaining theme and category selections).
- **Data Retention & Erasure Rights:**
  - Order records are kept only as long as necessary for support resolution, replacement verification, and basic accounting.
  - Customers can request the deletion or correction of their contact records at any time by emailing `portfoliostore99@gmail.com`.
